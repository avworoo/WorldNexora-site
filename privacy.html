Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What information we collect

When you use the contact form on this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, company or organisation (if given), the reason for your enquiry, the subject and your message, together with the date and time of your submission. We do not collect more personal information than is necessary to respond to you.

Why we collect it

We collect this information for one purpose: to understand and respond to your enquiry. Depending on the reason you select, your enquiry may be handled by the team responsible for partnerships, investment, media, careers or product support.

How contact form information is used

  • Submissions are processed by Formspree, our form-delivery provider, and sent to an official WorldNexora email address.
  • Your information is used only to respond to your enquiry and any follow-up related to it.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Formspree processes the information submitted through the contact form to provide the delivery service. You can read the Formspree Privacy Policy for more information.

Analytics

If this website uses analytics, they are privacy-conscious and used only to understand general visitor numbers, popular pages, broad location (country or region), and device types. Where analytics cookies are used, they are described in our Cookie Policy.

How long we keep information

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any reasonable follow-up, after which it is deleted.

Contacting us about your data

If you would like to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have provided, contact us at contact@wnexora.com.
